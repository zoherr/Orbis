# Orbis Design System

## Brand direction
- Product name: Orbis
- Core idea: a 2D metaverse for communities, collaboration, and personal identity
- Tone: warm, optimistic, expressive, and modern
- Primary message: Create Your Space. Find Your Orbit.
- Tagline treatment: use FC Fast for short punchy lines and brand moments; Graphik Regular for all UI and body copy

## Visual language
- Use a light, clean background with white or warm off-white surfaces
- Keep content airy: generous spacing, minimal density, strong hierarchy
- Use rounded corners and soft-edged cards to keep the product friendly and approachable
- Add orbit-inspired motifs, circular layouts, and layered depth to reinforce the “space/community” concept

## Color palette
- Lime neon: `#d3f625`
- Sky blue: `#5fa3ff`
- Deep orbit blue: `#084ba7`
- Background: `#f7f7f4` or white `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#111827` or near-black `#0d172a`
- Use lime as accent, blue as anchor/primary, and white surfaces to keep it clean

## Typography
- Headings and interface: Graphik Regular
- Taglines, short brand lines, and display moments: FC Fast Bold
- Keep taglines short, punchy, and energetic
- Examples:
  - Create Your Space. Find Your Orbit.
  - Together, In Your Orbit.
  - A virtual workspace that feels refreshingly human
  - Group chat that’s all fun & games
  - The place where your circle really connects

## UI patterns
- Primary actions: lime fill with deep blue hover or text for contrast
- Secondary actions: border + white background + subtle shadow
- Cards: white or warm off-white with blue accent borders, rounded corners, restrained gradients
- Use 2–3 accent colors max in a single section so the brand stays clean and elevated

## Layout rules
- Keep the hero structured with a clear left-to-right story: value, CTA, proof, and product visualization
- Use a calm but energetic layout: strong headline, short supporting text, 2 CTAs, quick social proof
- Avoid clutter; the experience should feel like a digital social space, not a rigid SaaS dashboard

## Implementation notes for the web app
- Favor clean white/off-white backgrounds with subtle blue or lime accents, not dark-mode-heavy interfaces
- For hero taglines, use FC Fast as a display font with tighter tracking and uppercase/lowercase mix where appropriate
- Use Graphik for the main copy to keep it readable and premium
- Apply gradients sparingly: only in orbit visuals, badges, and highlights

## Approved tagline directions
- Create Your Space. Find Your Orbit.
- Together, In Your Orbit.
- Your digital world, built for human connection
- Where communities become orbiting communities
- Live, create, and connect in your own orbit

## Brand principles
- The product should feel social, playful, collaborative, and spacious
- Human warmth matters more than technical density
- The UI should feel like the future of online gathering: expressive, thoughtful, and alive
